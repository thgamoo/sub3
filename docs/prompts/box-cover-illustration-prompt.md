- 능력 카드 7종: 검, 매력(부채), 마법(지팡이), 선혈(핏방울), 신성(십자가), 독약, 어둠

- 캐릭터 카드: 스탯 패널이나 숫자 없이 초상 중심 카드로 표현한다

## 커버 일러스트 생성 프롬프트

Use case: illustration-story
Asset type: board game box cover illustration, before typography and packaging layout
Primary request: Create a dramatic Korean romance-fantasy board game cover illustration for "삼류 악녀는 싫어!", focused on Charlotte walking toward the guillotine.
Input image roles:
- Jeanne reference: pink-haired heroine in academy uniform, emotional and innocent.
- Siegfried reference: black-haired northern grand duke's son with red eyes, cold black armor and fur.
- Alec reference: sharp-eyed desert prince with turban and dark blue-gold clothing.
- Gaius reference: blond imperial crown prince in white and purple-gold imperial dress.
- Charlotte reference: blonde noblewoman with sharp blue eyes, curled twin-roll aristocratic hairstyle, long curled sidelocks, side-swept bangs, lace-and-ribbon noble design language. For this execution scene her clothing should be stripped down and humbled, but her recognizable Charlotte hairstyle and eye shape must remain.

Scene/backdrop: A grand public execution square in a romance-fantasy imperial capital, late afternoon stormlight. Stone steps and a clear walking path lead in one continuous direction toward a guillotine at the left-rear or center-rear. The guillotine must face the same direction as Charlotte's movement so the destination and her path visually align. Nobles and guards are implied as blurred silhouettes, not detailed side characters. The mood is tragic, theatrical, and accusatory.

Subject and exact staging:
1. Charlotte must be the visual focus. She is in the foreground or central midground, walking in the same direction as the guillotine entrance with a devastated expression. Her identity must be unmistakably Charlotte: keep her blonde twin-roll hairstyle, curled sidelocks, side-swept bangs, sharp blue eyes, and proud aristocratic eye shape. Her hair may be slightly disheveled for the execution scene, but only a few loose strands and loosened curls; do not make her hair fully loose, wild, or unrecognizable. Her execution outfit is much plainer and humbler than a noble ball gown: a simple pale dress, minimal lace, no ornate jewelry, no fan, no book, no accessories in her hands. She is not dead, not injured, and not restrained graphically; the tragedy is emotional.
2. Jeanne is behind and to one side, crying but resolute, being held by Siegfried only. Jeanne should have tears on her face, but her expression is firm and convinced, giving the feeling that she has just condemned and defeated the villainess rather than pitying Charlotte.
3. Siegfried holds Jeanne protectively. He has black hair, red eyes, black noble armor or military coat with fur, stern expression.
4. Alec stands beside Siegfried with arms crossed and an angry expression. He has a turban, tan skin, dark curly hair, sharp eyes, and blue-gold desert prince clothing.
5. Gaius stands slightly forward from the group, pointing accusingly at Charlotte. He is blond, blue-eyed, wearing white and purple-gold imperial clothing.

Style/medium: high-end Korean romance-fantasy anime illustration, premium board game box art, painterly rendering, cinematic composition, ornate costumes, expressive faces, polished commercial key art.

Composition/framing: Landscape box-cover composition, 3:2 or 16:10 ratio. Charlotte is the focal point and largest readable emotional subject, but avoid pushing her so far forward that the rest of the scene loses context. The guillotine must be visible but not grotesque, and its orientation must match Charlotte's walking direction. Jeanne, Siegfried, Alec, and Gaius form a secondary accusing group behind or to the side. Use strong diagonal movement from Charlotte toward the guillotine, with Gaius's pointing gesture directing the viewer's eye back to Charlotte. Leave some clean negative space near the top or bottom for later title placement, but do not render text.

Lighting/mood: dramatic late-afternoon backlight, pale gold and gray storm clouds, theatrical rim light on Charlotte, cooler shadows around the accusers, tragic and tense.

Color palette: Charlotte in plain pale ivory, faded lavender, and muted gray-gold, with less ornament than usual; Jeanne in soft pink and navy academy colors; Siegfried in black, silver, and red; Alec in deep blue, white, and gold; Gaius in white, purple, and gold; background in stone gray, muted rose, and storm gold.

Materials/textures: simple cotton or dull satin for Charlotte's humbled execution dress, limited lace, polished armor, rich brocade on the male leads, carved stone, parchment-like atmospheric dust, subtle rose petals in the air.

Text: no text, no logo, no watermark.

Constraints:
- Charlotte walking toward the guillotine is the main focus.
- Charlotte's walking direction and the guillotine's facing direction must align clearly.
- Charlotte wears a plain humbled execution dress, not a lavish noble gown.
- Charlotte's recognizable twin-roll hairstyle, curled sidelocks, side-swept bangs, sharp blue eyes, and aristocratic eye shape must remain.
- Charlotte's hair is only slightly messy: a few loose strands and loosened curls, not fully loose or wild.
- Charlotte has empty hands: no book, no fan, no accessory.
- Jeanne is held by Siegfried only.
- Jeanne cries but looks firm and resolute, as if she has condemned the villainess.
- Alec is next to Siegfried, arms crossed, angry.
- Gaius is forward and pointing at Charlotte.
- Do not include Giovanni.
- Keep Charlotte, Jeanne, Siegfried, Alec, and Gaius distinct and readable.
- No gore, no severed body parts, no graphic violence.
- No extra named characters in focus.
- No modern clothing, no photorealism, no chibi style.
- No title text in the illustration; typography will be added later on the box cover.
