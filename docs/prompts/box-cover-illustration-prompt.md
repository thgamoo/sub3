`docs/rules/tokens.md` 기준으로 보드게임 제작물 목업에는 아래 요소만 사용한다.

- 능력 카드 7종: 검, 매력(부채), 마법(지팡이), 선혈(핏방울), 신성(십자가), 독약, 어둠

## 커버 일러스트 생성 프롬프트

Use case: illustration-story
Asset type: board game box cover illustration, before typography and packaging layout
Primary request: Create a dramatic Korean romance-fantasy board game cover illustration for "삼류 악녀는 싫어!", focused on Charlotte standing isolated from the heroine and male leads after her scheme has failed. Do not use a guillotine, execution device, scaffold, hanging platform, blade, noose, or explicit execution imagery.
Input image roles:
- Jeanne reference: pink-haired heroine in academy uniform, emotional and innocent.
- Siegfried reference: black-haired northern grand duke's son with red eyes, cold black armor and fur.
- Alec reference: sharp-eyed desert prince with turban and dark blue-gold clothing.
- Gaius reference: blond imperial crown prince in white and purple-gold imperial dress.
- Charlotte reference: blonde noblewoman with sharp blue eyes, curled twin-roll aristocratic hairstyle, long curled sidelocks, side-swept bangs, lace-and-ribbon noble design language. For this cover scene she should still read as a defeated aristocratic villainess, but not as an execution prisoner; her recognizable Charlotte hairstyle, eye shape, and noble silhouette must remain.

Scene/backdrop: A grand romance-fantasy imperial academy court or palace terrace after a public confrontation, late afternoon stormlight. Charlotte stands apart in a moderate pocket of negative space, visually separated from Jeanne and the male leads by body language, cold stone paving, drifting rose petals, and opposing light direction, not by an exaggerated empty distance. Nobles and guards are implied as blurred silhouettes only, not detailed side characters. The mood is tragic, theatrical, tense, and emotionally accusatory without showing any execution device or graphic punishment.

Subject and exact staging:
1. Charlotte must be the visual focus without becoming an oversized close-up. She is in the foreground or central midground as a readable three-quarter body or half-body figure, not a giant cropped face, not an extreme portrait crop. Her head should be only moderately larger than the heads in the Jeanne/Siegfried/Alec/Gaius group, consistent with normal perspective. She stands alone or takes a small stalled step away from the others, exhaling a deep frustrated sigh because events did not unfold as she intended. Her mouth is slightly open in a quiet sigh, her brows are tense, and her eyes are dry or only faintly glassy; do not put visible tears on Charlotte's cheeks. Her expression is wounded, cornered, exhausted, and bitterly disappointed, not screaming, not crying, not physically defeated. Her posture should show controlled aristocratic pride beginning to crack: one hand lightly at her chest or lowered at her side, shoulders subtly dropped, gaze turned slightly away from the group. Her hair may be slightly disheveled, but only a few loose strands and loosened curls; do not make her hair fully loose, wild, or unrecognizable. Her clothing is less triumphant than a ball gown but still noble: pale ivory, faded lavender, muted gray-gold, restrained lace and ribbons, no ornate jewelry overload, no fan, no book, no accessories in her hands. She is not dead, not injured, not imprisoned, not bound, and not facing an execution device; the tragedy is emotional isolation.
2. Jeanne is physically close to Siegfried, Alec, and Gaius as part of one tight secondary group behind and to one side. Jeanne is held only by Siegfried, with her back mostly facing the viewer. Jeanne should not face the camera frontally. Show a slight side profile over Siegfried's arm or shoulder, with visible tears and a trembling mouth, as if she is trying not to collapse after the confrontation. Her body language is fragile and shaken rather than accusatory.
3. Siegfried holds Jeanne protectively from behind or at her side. He has black hair, red eyes, black noble armor or military coat with fur, stern expression. His attention is on Jeanne first, reinforcing Charlotte's emotional isolation.
4. Alec stands close beside Siegfried within the same compact group, arms crossed and angry. He has a turban, tan skin, dark curly hair, sharp eyes, and blue-gold desert prince clothing.
5. Gaius stands close to the group and only slightly forward, pointing accusingly at Charlotte without breaking the group's tight formation. He is blond, blue-eyed, wearing white and purple-gold imperial clothing.

Style/medium: high-end Korean romance-fantasy anime illustration, premium board game box art, painterly rendering, cinematic composition, ornate costumes, expressive faces, polished commercial key art.

Charlotte is the focal point and largest readable emotional subject, but she should not dominate the frame as a huge cropped face; keep her visible as a three-quarter body or half-body figure. Jeanne, Siegfried, Alec, and Gaius form one compact secondary group behind or to the side, standing close enough that they read as a unified party. Color palette: Charlotte in plain pale ivory, faded lavender, and muted gray-gold, with less ornament than usual; Jeanne in soft pink and navy academy colors; Siegfried in black, silver, and red; Alec in deep blue, white, and gold; Gaius in white, purple, and gold; background in stone gray, muted rose, and storm gold.
