# Event Illustration Prompts

## Tumblbug 1:1 Banner Recommendation

1:1 배너 키비주얼로는 **1번 샬롯/잔느 무도회 장면**을 1순위로 추천한다. 캐릭터 감정이 즉시 읽히고, 로맨스 판타지 장르성이 강하며, 말풍선 없이도 "악녀 역할을 강요받는 샬롯"이라는 핵심 컨셉을 전달하기 좋다.

2순위는 **3번 피의 비를 내리는 재앙**이다. 임팩트는 가장 강하지만 텀블벅 첫인상으로는 다소 어둡고 호러 톤이 강할 수 있어, 배너보다는 멸망 시나리오 운명 카드나 섹션 비주얼에 더 적합하다.

생성 시 공통 조건:
- 1:1 square composition, suitable for Tumblbug campaign banner and event card illustration.
- Romance fantasy webnovel anime illustration, polished collectible card art.
- Clear central silhouette, readable at thumbnail size.
- No logo, no watermark, no UI text.
- Avoid readable dialogue or narration text unless explicitly approved before generation.

## 1. Charlotte Harasses Jeanne At The Ball

```text
Use case: illustration-story
Asset type: 1:1 Tumblbug banner candidate and fate event card illustration
Primary request: Charlotte publicly humiliates Jeanne at the center of a noble ball. Outwardly Charlotte must look cold, mocking, and contemptuous, as if thinking "how dare a lowborn commoner stand here"; only a small textless thought-image bubble reveals her hidden panic under fate's compulsion.
Scene/backdrop: A glittering aristocratic ballroom at night, chandeliers, polished marble floor, the confrontation happening clearly in the open center of the ballroom. Blurred noble guests surround the scene: some ignore it and keep dancing or talking, while several watching couples pause and laugh at Jeanne with openly mocking expressions.
Subject: Charlotte, a refined haughty young noble lady with bright golden blonde long twin vertical drill curls and clear blue eyes, wears an elegant deep royal blue and white noble dress and holds a folding fan. Jeanne, a pure heroine with soft pink short bob hair and big clear pink eyes, wears a modest neat academy uniform adapted for a ball.
Style/medium: Romance fantasy webnovel anime illustration, polished collectible card art, expressive character acting.
Composition/framing: Square 1:1 composition, side-view stage-like framing. Charlotte stands on the left and Jeanne stands on the right, both shown at the same body scale and similar full-body or three-quarter-body size. They face each other in the ballroom center. Charlotte raises her chin with arrogant contempt, wearing a cold sneering smile with one corner of her mouth lifted and sharp dismissive eyes. Charlotte's right hand holds an open folding fan, lightly covering her mouth. Charlotte's left hand points accusingly at Jeanne in a clear rude finger-pointing gesture. Jeanne faces her, hurt and confused, slightly teary-eyed with one or two small tears, but not physically smaller. Two plain-looking noble young ladies stand near Charlotte as mocking hanger-on friends, less beautiful and less detailed than Charlotte, laughing and looking down on Jeanne. A few blurred extra nobles form a loose ring around them, with some uninterested guests in motion and some watching couples openly smirking or laughing.
Lighting/mood: Sparkling ballroom light with emotional tension, beautiful but uncomfortable.
Color palette: Royal blue, white, gold, soft pink, warm chandelier light.
Text (verbatim): no text.
Constraints: No readable speech bubble text. The hidden panic should appear only as a small symbolic thought-image bubble near Charlotte, showing a tiny panicked version of Charlotte or abstract trembling marks, without words. Charlotte's real visible face must remain a cold mocking sneer, not a panicked expression. The scene must show the contradiction between her outward contempt and the textless panic symbol. Charlotte's right hand must cover her mouth with the fan, and Charlotte's left hand must point at Jeanne. The two noble hanger-on girls must read as petty mocking extras, not main heroines, and should not be prettier or more eye-catching than Charlotte. Background guests must stay mob-like and low-detail, not detailed named characters.
Avoid: No modern clothing, no armor, no weapons, no gore, no watermark, no logo, no extra main characters beyond two mocking noble-girl extras, no readable letters, no dialogue text.
```

## 2. Lucrezia Manipulates Zenobia And Irene Into Battle

```text
Use case: illustration-story
Asset type: 1:1 fate event card illustration
Primary request: Lucrezia controls Zenobia and Irene, forcing them to fight Jeanne's party: Jeanne, Siegfried, Charlotte, Augustus, and Alec.
Scene/backdrop: A dramatic cathedral courtyard at dusk, cracked marble, pale roses, stained-glass light, faint ominous control threads spreading from Lucrezia.
Subject: Lucrezia is a cold beautiful noble villainess with golden blonde vertical drill curls, narrow icy blue eyes, a pale violet gown, and an ornate jeweled fan. Zenobia is a stoic holy knight commander with copper tanned skin, long brown hair, dark eyes, and a plain silver half-mask covering the burned right side of her face. Irene is a saintess candidate with pale yellow hair, long bangs covering her eyes, faint golden eyes, simple white priestess vestments, and a plain white-gold bishop's mitre. Opposing them are Jeanne with pink bob hair, Siegfried with black hair and red eyes, Charlotte with blonde drill curls, Augustus with blond hair and blue eyes, and Alec with a jeweled turban and sharp eyes.
Style/medium: Romance fantasy webnovel anime illustration, cinematic collectible card art.
Composition/framing: Square 1:1 battle tableau. Lucrezia appears elevated in the rear center as the manipulator, small but visually dominant. Zenobia and Irene occupy the foreground as controlled attackers. Jeanne's party is grouped defensively opposite them as readable silhouettes, with Jeanne most visible.
Lighting/mood: Holy light corrupted by violet shadow, tragic forced-conflict mood.
Color palette: Violet, white, gold, ash gray, muted cathedral colors.
Constraints: Prioritize readable faction composition over perfect detail for every face. Zenobia and Irene must look controlled, conflicted, or trance-like, not willingly cruel. Lucrezia's control must be visible through subtle magical threads or fan gesture.
Avoid: No excessive gore, no crosses as explicit symbols, no guns, no modern clothing, no watermark, no logo, no unreadable crowd clutter.
```

## 4. Grace And Marie Fight While Daisy Watches

```text
Use case: illustration-story
Asset type: 1:1 fate event card illustration
Primary request: Grace, the great merchant and pirate, fights Marie, the poison master, while Daisy hides behind a rock and watches.
Scene/backdrop: A rocky sea cliff near a smuggler's cove at stormy sunset, ocean spray, scattered merchant crates and broken potion glass.
Subject: Grace has short curly red bob hair, a prominent golden monocle, a confident smirk, a stylish captain coat with gold trim, jeweled rings, and a cutlass. Marie has wild red-and-green two-tone hair, hollow pitch-black eyes without pupils, a wide unhinged grin, dark alchemist robes, and glowing green poison vials. Daisy is a quiet maid with brown hair in a bun, brown eyes, and a black-and-white Victorian maid uniform, hidden behind a large rock watching tensely.
Style/medium: Romance fantasy webnovel anime illustration, action card art, dynamic but readable.
Composition/framing: Square 1:1 composition. Grace and Marie clash diagonally in the foreground: cutlass and poison vapor crossing. Daisy is clearly visible in the lower side background, half-hidden behind a rock, observing without joining.
Lighting/mood: Stormy sea action, dangerous but stylish.
Color palette: Sea blue, storm gray, crimson hair, toxic green, warm gold accents.
Constraints: All three characters must be visible. Daisy must be hiding and watching, not fighting. Marie's poison should read as toxic vapor and vials, not voodoo objects.
Avoid: No dolls, no skulls, no bone charms, no gore, no guns, no modern clothing, no watermark, no logo.
```

Color palette: Moonlit blue, ink black, muted gold, soft pink accent from Jeanne.
